Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Crypto Profit Calculator ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address (for email/password accounts) or basic profile information provided by Google (for Google Sign-In), via Firebase Authentication.
  • Calculation History: We store the crypto profit calculations you perform (coin name, amounts, prices, and results) linked to your account. This data is stored using Firebase services to allow you to view your history.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage and diagnostic data through Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app's performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your account and calculation history.
  • To improve app performance, troubleshoot bugs, and develop new features.
  • To display advertisements through third-party ad networks (if applicable).

3. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication, analytics, and data storage. Google Privacy Policy .
  • CoinGecko API: Used for live cryptocurrency price data. No personal data is sent to CoinGecko.
  • Google Play Services: Required for core app functionality on Android devices.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security rules to ensure only you can access your own account data. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated calculation history at any time using the "Delete Account" option in the app's Settings screen. This action is permanent and removes your data from our systems.
