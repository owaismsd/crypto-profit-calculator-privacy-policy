Delete Your Crypto Profit Calculator Account

If you would like to delete your Crypto Profit Calculator account and associated data, you can request account deletion using the instructions below.

What will be deleted?

When your account is deleted, the data associated with your account will be deleted, including:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your email address associated with the account
  • Your crypto calculation history
  • Other account-associated data stored by the app
Important:

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and associated data have been deleted, they cannot be recovered.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, send an email to:

2codestudio.support@gmail.com

Please use the following subject:

Account Deletion Request — Crypto Profit Calculator

In your email, provide the email address associated with your Crypto Profit Calculator account so that we can identify the account and process your request.

For security purposes, we may need to verify that the request belongs to the account owner before completing the deletion.

Delete your account from the app

If you have access to the app, you can also delete your account directly from:

Settings → Delete Account

Need help?

If you have questions about account or data deletion, contact us at:
